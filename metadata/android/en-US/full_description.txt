<p>A read-only Android client for the infrastructure you already run yourself. StackMate connects to your own Grafana and Portainer instances and shows their current state on your phone.</p><h2>What it does</h2><ul><li>One glance across every server you add: firing alerts, container state, health checks, and environment availability</li><li>Grafana: server health, version, and active alerts with severity and timestamps</li><li>Portainer: container state, Docker health check results, and offline environments</li><li>A background check every 30 minutes with a low-key notification when something needs attention</li></ul><h2>What it deliberately does not do</h2><p>StackMate is a viewer, not a monitor. Your existing servers do the watching; this app shows you what they report. It never changes anything on your infrastructure, so it needs no write access to your Grafana or Docker socket.</p><h2>Privacy</h2><ul><li>No account, no sign-up, no analytics, and no crash reporting</li><li>No network requests to anything except the servers you configure</li><li>Credentials are encrypted on-device with the Android Keystore</li><li>Nothing is stored on a Kituwa IT server, because there isn't one</li></ul><h2>Honest about silence</h2><p>If a server cannot be reached, StackMate says so explicitly and timestamps the last data it received. It never renders an outage as a healthy green checkmark, because in a homelab the most dangerous signal is silence.</p><h2>Getting started</h2><p>For Grafana, create a service account token with the <code>alerts:read</code> permission under Administration, then Users and access, then Service accounts. For Portainer, use your ordinary username and password and StackMate will exchange them for a short-lived token. Plain HTTP works, so a reverse proxy with a self-signed certificate is fine.</p><h2>Who made this</h2><p>Brought to you by Kituwa IT. If you would rather have someone else keep an eye on your infrastructure, that is what we do. StackMate stays free and self-hosted either way, and it is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later, so you are free to inspect it, change it, and run your own copy.</p>
